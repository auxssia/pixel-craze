About DSPIN

DSPIN goes beyond traditional consulting. It guides companies through market entry, then helps put the strategy to work on the ground.

DSPIN Consulting is a specialised firm for global companies establishing and expanding in India. Its focus is the Indian business landscape: the regulatory framework, the market dynamics, and what it takes to make an entry run smoothly.

The goal is practical: business expansion, client acquisition, sales leadership, and expert advice, all aimed at growth in the Indian market.

What every engagement is built on

Client-centric focus
The approach is shaped around each client's needs, so the advice stays relevant to the business it is for.
Expertise and innovation
Deep industry knowledge, brought together with new thinking about entering and growing in India.
Results and long-term partnership
A commitment to delivering measurable results, and to partnerships that last beyond the first engagement.
