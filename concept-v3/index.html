Questions parents ask

Is this a test of my child?

No. The four questions are about what you are looking for. The suggestion is a starting point that you can change or discuss with us.

Does it cost anything?

The questions and the suggestion are free. Fees for classes and products are shown on the EduNique and Skillioneer pages. TBC

Why do you ask for my WhatsApp number?

So the right team can send you the suggestion and answer your questions. We use it for this enquiry only.

Can my child do both?

You can, but we suggest starting with one. The questions help you pick which.
